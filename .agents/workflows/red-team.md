# /red-team

Objetivo: tentar derrubar uma recomendação antes de fortalecê-la.

```text
1. Freeze the current recommendation and its supporting metric.
2. Identify the three uncertainties most capable of changing the decision.
3. For each uncertainty, define a test and failure criterion before seeing the result.
4. Test alternative explanations and composition effects.
5. State whether the conclusion survives, weakens, reverses or becomes untestable.
6. Record the result in the decision log.
7. Do not rewrite previous artifacts to hide the correction.
```

Prioritize decision-changing tests over exhaustive testing.
